Support

Most things you need are below. For everything else, write to madgesmy@protonmail.com — we aim to reply within five business days.

Quickest way to reach us

Email madgesmy@protonmail.com with:

  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
  • Countdown version (in the app: Settings → About)
  • A short description of what you tried and what happened

Frequently asked

How do I restore a previous Pro purchase?

Open Countdown → Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. If nothing happens, double-check the Apple ID in Settings app → [your name] → Media & Purchases.

I bought Pro and the lock icon is still showing.

Try Settings → Restore purchases first. If that doesn't help, fully quit Countdown (swipe up from the app switcher) and relaunch. Still stuck? Email us with your purchase date and the email tied to your Apple ID.

The widget isn't updating / is showing an old day count.

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule — usually once an hour and at midnight. To force-refresh: long-press the widget → Remove → re-add it. If it still shows yesterday's count after midnight, please email us.

How do I add a Lock-Screen widget?

Long-press your Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → Add Widgets → Countdown. Lock-Screen widgets are a Pro feature; on the Free tier you'll see an "Upgrade to Pro" tile that opens the app.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is on your device. We don't sync to the cloud and we don't run analytics. If you delete the app, your events go with it. See the privacy policy for full detail.

I want a refund.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Countdown purchase, and submit a refund request. Apple decides; we don't.

Can I export my events?

Not yet. Right now Countdown is offline-first with no export feature. If this matters to you, please write — we want to hear which formats people actually need (calendar invite, CSV, JSON) before we ship one.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Countdown → Settings → Delete all data. This removes every event from this device and cannot be undone. Notifications you've scheduled get cancelled automatically.

System requirements

  • iOS 16 or later for Lock-Screen widgets
  • iOS 14 or later for home-screen widgets
  • An Apple ID for purchases and Pro restore
